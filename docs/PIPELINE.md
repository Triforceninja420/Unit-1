# PIPELINE.md — industrial Unit-1: talk first, move honest, chores later

Design owned by Craig. Started 2026-09-17.

This is the followable path for the **industrial hang**
(`INDUSTRIAL.md`): platinum-silicone body, stand-locked skeleton,
12 V heat, local brain.

It is **not** the public Frame kit. The Frame already shuffles at 8–14 kg
(`FRAME.md`, `BUILD.md`). Do not put this 32–38 kg skin on that rail.

House chores are a north-star joke until there is a manipulator, a
balance loop, and a 35 N story that survives a dropped mug. Talk is
near. Assisted motion is near. Free walk and housework are not this coupon.

## Lane C — motion (W gates from INDUSTRIAL.md)

Wanting her to walk the hall does not change hip moment.
32–38 kg on a friction / ratchet skeleton is a stand machine until W4.

| Gate | You do | Pass when | Walk claim? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| W0 | Dressed mass, CoM height, foot polygon on the card | Numbers exist | No |
| W1 | Three-pad FSR (or cheap sole map) under one foot or the stand plate. Lean and log pressure center | You can draw where load sits | No |
| W2 | Ceiling hoist or boom. Padded straps on the **torso cage**. Two-person lift rule still on | She cannot free-fall 35 kg | No |
| W3 | Assisted steps. Hoist takes **≥ 50 %** of dressed weight. Knees may ratchet between plants | She planted the other foot without collapsing | **Motion only** |
| W4 | New hip / ankle actuator class sized from W0 numbers. Not DS-class hobby. Not DittoBot EPM. New fuse + stall-A row | Actuator holds dressed moment in single-support **on the hoist** | Still not free walk |
| W5 | Local MCU balance: IMU + sole wrench/FSR fusion. No cloud in the loop. Boom can slacken only after N captured falls-that-did-not-happen | Written test protocol + card | First *candidate* for a later walk SKU |

Boom-on or hoist-on steps after W3 are **motion**.
They are not “the industrial version walks.”

Silicone + foam is a lossy spring: kinder heel strike, worse state estimate.
Assisted gait uses that. Free gait has to model it.

## Lane D — house chores (parked, on purpose)

“Do the dishes / fetch a glass / make the bed” needs all of:

- W5-class balance, or a rolling base so she is not a 35 kg biped in the kitchen
- Hands that can grasp without exceeding **35 N** on a human who gets in the way
- A task planner that is **not** allowed to override E-stop, brownout, or heater fuse
- Dry silicone, no wet tunnels, no unattended heat while she “works”
- Adult workspace only. No child in the loop, no “nanny” variant

Until those exist, chores are a punchline and a north star, not a build step.
If you want a shop helper sooner, that is the **public Frame** with mitts,
or a wheeled cart that carries the industrial hang’s *voice box* — not the
35 kg body chasing a sponge.
