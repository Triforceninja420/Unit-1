# OPTIONAL_SHELL.md — later cover (not the kit)

Design owned by Craig. This file is **not** required to build a C-3PO-style droid.

The public project is the Frame. Anyone can build that.
This page is only if you later hang a different cover on a finished, card-filled armature.

## Rules that do not move

- The Frame walk claim does not transfer to a heavy soft shell.
- 32–38 kg silicone stays poseable. No dressed walk on hobby servos.
- 41 °C skin cap and 35 N pinch interrupt still apply if you add heat or clamps.
- Adult-only if the cover is adult. Childlike covers are not a variant.
- No companion claim until that stack’s own measurement rows are filled.

## What you may hang

- PETG / foam protocol plates (preferred, < 2 kg) — still the public kit
- Bought soft body as a *static* costume on a locked stand
- Local voice with a different constitution

## What you may not hang on the gait rail

- A full doll asked to shuffle
- Mains heaters
- Cloud girlfriend as the servo brain

Path 1 heat / sleeve / Jetson notes from the first draft live here as research, not as the README. Frame numbers stay in `FRAME.md`.
